Wobble Tower Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Wobble Tower is a clay stacking game for Root, made by OddBitty. This page explains what information the game keeps, why it keeps it, and who can see it.

What the game keeps

When you finish a game, Wobble Tower saves your score so it can show your best and the community leaderboard:

  • Your best scores: your best score for each tower type, mode and level, saved with your Root user ID.
  • Leaderboards: the top scores for each tower type, mode and level. Each entry has your Root user ID, your Root nickname (updated each time you finish a game), your best score and the date you set it. Up to 25 entries are kept on each leaderboard.
  • Server log: the game's log notes your nickname when you open the game and when you set a new best. The log helps us fix problems.

What the game does not keep

  • The game does not save your profile picture or any other details from your Root profile, apart from your nickname on the leaderboard.
  • The game does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • The game does not send your information to any service outside Root.

Where your information is stored

Your scores and the leaderboards are stored in the storage that Root provides for the game. Each community where the game is installed has its own leaderboards. Root's own privacy policy covers how Root stores and protects that data.

Your device also remembers a few choices, such as whether the sound and music are on and which level and tower type you last picked. These stay on your device.

Who can see it

  • Members of the community can see the top 10 scores on each leaderboard, with the nickname of each player.
  • If you are in the top 25, you can see your own place on that leaderboard, even when you are not in the top 10.

Removing your information

A new score only replaces your saved best when it is higher. If you would like to know what the game holds about you, or ask us to remove your scores, email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes what it keeps, we will update this page and the date at the top.
